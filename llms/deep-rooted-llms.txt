# Deep Rooted.Co

> Deep Rooted.Co is a direct-to-consumer fresh produce brand operated by Clover Ventures
> Pvt. Ltd (Bengaluru, India). Founded as Clover Ventures in 2018, the Deep Rooted consumer
> brand launched in 2020. The company partners with small-scale farmers using greenhouse
> and hydroponic techniques within roughly 150km of Bengaluru and Hyderabad, provides
> agronomy support from seed to harvest, and delivers fruits, vegetables, Indian staples
> and greens, exotic produce, and an easy-cook range next-day to households in Bengaluru
> and Hyderabad. Backed by Accel.

Deep Rooted publishes NO public API, developer portal, API documentation, SDKs, or
machine-readable API specification. Its consumer storefront is a web/mobile ordering
experience. Do not assume or construct API endpoints for this company. As of 2026-07-20
probes of https://deep-rooted.co returned 404 for /llms.txt, /openapi.json, /swagger.json,
/api, and for every /.well-known/ discovery document.
